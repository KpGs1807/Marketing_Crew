# Target Audience Segments for AI Excel Automation Tool (SMEs)

Based on the market research highlighting manual data entry pain points and a lack of advanced Excel/VBA skills, we have identified the following target audience segments within SMEs:

## 1. Finance & Accounting Teams
*   **Role:** Financial controllers, accountants, bookkeepers.
*   **Pain Points:** Time-consuming manual reconciliation, repetitive data entry, high risk of human error in financial reporting, lack of advanced Excel/VBA expertise.
*   **Value Proposition:** Automate reconciliation and financial reporting, ensure data accuracy, and free up time for strategic financial analysis.

## 2. Operations & Administrative Managers
*   **Role:** Office managers, operations coordinators, administrative assistants.
*   **Pain Points:** Managing large datasets for inventory, scheduling, or client databases; repetitive copy-pasting tasks; slow processing times.
*   **Value Proposition:** Drastically reduce time spent on daily administrative data tasks, minimize formatting errors, and streamline operational workflows.

## 3. Data-Heavy Business Analysts (Non-Technical)
*   **Role:** Marketing analysts, sales operations, small business owners performing their own analytics.
*   **Pain Points:** Struggling to manipulate data from multiple sources in Excel, manual cleaning of messy data, inability to leverage complex Excel features.
*   **Value Proposition:** Simplify complex data preparation and cleaning processes without needing coding knowledge, enabling faster data-driven decision-making.
