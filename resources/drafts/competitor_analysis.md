# Competitor Analysis: AI Excel Automation (2026)

Based on market research, the following 3 competitors have been identified as key players in the AI-powered Excel and data automation space for SMEs:

## 1. Microsoft Copilot for Microsoft 365
- **Positioning:** The industry standard for enterprise and SME integration. Deeply embedded within the Excel interface.
- **Key Features:** Generative AI for formula creation, data visualization, trend spotting, and automated reporting.
- **Pricing:** Typically subscription-based, included in M365 enterprise/business plans (approx. $30/user/month).

## 2. Ajelix
- **Positioning:** An "AI workspace" specifically built for data-heavy business workflows and Excel automation.
- **Key Features:** Excel formula generator, VBA script automation, data formatting, and specialized spreadsheet intelligence tools.
- **Pricing:** Tiered subscription model (Free, Pro, Business) targeting individual power users to small teams.

## 3. Pelles.ai
- **Positioning:** A specialized AI plug-in designed to streamline spreadsheet workflows and interact with data using natural language.
- **Key Features:** Focuses on reducing manual spreadsheet drudgery, intelligent data interaction, and workflow automation.
- **Pricing:** Often operates on a freemium or usage-based model for SMEs.
